# Operations

## Purpose

This document defines how production AI systems are operated after deployment.

Operations ensure that deployed systems remain available, reliable, secure, and maintainable throughout their lifecycle.

## Objectives

- Maintain system availability.
- Standardize routine maintenance.
- Reduce operational risk.
- Document recurring procedures.
- Establish operational discipline.

## Operational Activities

- Service Health Checks
- Log Management
- Performance Monitoring
- Package Updates
- Dependency Management
- Database Maintenance
- SSL Renewal Verification
- Backup Verification
- Resource Monitoring
- User Support

## Operational Frequency

Daily

Weekly

Monthly

As Needed
